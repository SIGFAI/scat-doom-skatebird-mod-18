# SuperIntelligent Cat: credits

Original code (ZScript), the 3D-to-sprite renders and the sky were made for this mod. Reused assets below.

## 3D models (rendered into the 8-angle Doom sprites)
- **Cube Pets** (cat, parrot, chick, fish models + colormap texture) by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), **CC0 1.0**.
  https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets. Used for every cat, the boss body, the skating birds, the player's wings and the thrown fish;
  the colormap was recoloured for the ginger, black, Siamese, creeper, boss and blue-bird variants.
- **Mini Skate** (skateboard model) by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), **CC0 1.0**. https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-skate.
  The thrown 360-flip board, the first-person board and the birds' fingerboards.
